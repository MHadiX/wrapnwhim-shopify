# Content and photographic direction

The theme bundles original AI-generated **demonstration imagery**, generated during this development task. There are no external stock-photo hotlinks. These images portray inspirational gifting scenes, not a claim about WrapnWhim's actual products, packaging, customer reviews or inventory.

- `gifting-editorial.webp`: navy/champagne gift-box editorial scene; 1536 × 1024, plus responsive derivatives and a mobile crop.
- `personal.webp`, `flowers.webp`, `chocolates.webp`, `giftbox.webp`, `lifestyle.webp`, `wedding.webp`: six coordinated 512 × 512 photographic panels extracted from a generated contact sheet, plus 320px derivatives.
- Total imagery is compressed WebP. Product and merchant-uploaded images use Shopify CDN responsive widths. Replace the 512px demo panels with higher-resolution merchant photography for larger merchandising areas.

The raw generation outputs remain outside the checkout. Image preparation scripts record the process and source paths for provenance, but are not needed to install or run the theme. All storefront image areas have theme-editor image pickers; collection and product images come from real Shopify resources. No generated catalog, price, rating or testimonial is presented as real.

Recommended photography: consistent warm window light; ivory stone/linen backgrounds; navy boxes and champagne ribbon; accurate product colors; natural materials; no embedded promotional text. Suggested uploads: desktop heroes ≥ 2000px wide, mobile heroes ≥ 1000px tall, product photos ≥ 1600px, collection/editorial cards ≥ 1200px. Provide descriptive image alt text in Admin.

## Example editor plan

Configure five hero slides: The Art of Thoughtful Gifting; Gifts Made Personal; Beautifully Wrapped, Beautifully Remembered; Celebrate Every Occasion; Curated Gift Boxes. Give each slide its own photography, mobile crop and real destination.

Connect the twelve category blocks and eleven occasion blocks to your actual collections. They are editable labels, not hardcoded collection identities. Select your featured and best-sellers collections. Point the personalization promotion to its actual collection and the box promotion to the published builder page. Upload inspiration-gallery photographs and only link genuine social profiles.

Add real support details and a factual brand story. Suggested About copy to adapt: “We believe a thoughtful gift can turn a small moment into a lasting memory. WrapnWhim brings together gifts, personal touches and beautiful presentation for the people who matter to you.” Do not claim years in business, awards, delivery guarantees or customer counts without evidence.

Leave the reviews section empty until you have genuine consented reviews, or keep the explicit demonstration label enabled on demonstration blocks. Shipping times, free-shipping offers, COD claims, payment-provider logos, policies and countdown promotions must reflect actual merchant settings.
